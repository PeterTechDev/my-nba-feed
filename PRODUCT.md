# NBA Feed

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

NBA fans catching up on their teams, including fans who watch games after they finish.

## Product Purpose

Bring team news, games, schedules, standings, highlights and community posts together, with control over when results are revealed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Fans can choose a focus team, follow multiple teams and configure score and headline protection. External destinations may contain spoilers. Data depends on third-party sources and may be delayed or unavailable.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the NBA Feed name and established interface. The About page is product first, followed by a short creator section; confirmed by the user on October 27, 2026.
