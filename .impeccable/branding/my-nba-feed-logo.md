At left, a simple amber #FBBF24 circular basketball mark with clean near-black seams. Two horizontal cuts on its right subtly suggest newsfeed rows. At right, the exact words "My NBA Feed" in bold contemporary white sans-serif type on a single line. Strong balance, precisely spaced letters, simple recognizable silhouette, refined sports editorial identity. Large logo centered in a wide landscape canvas with sensible margins. Strictly flat 2D vector-style design: pure solid color shapes, immaculate smooth clean edges, absolutely no texture, no speckles, no distress, no gradients, no bevels, no glow, no shadows, no outlines. This is a clean digital interface logo, not merchandise or streetwear. Do not use the official NBA logo or a player silhouette. No taglines, extra text, mockups, alternate logos, or presentation elements. Opaque solid black background, not transparent.
